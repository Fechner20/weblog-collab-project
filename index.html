Spotify Through the Years.

Spotify has gone through a lot of changes to get to where it is today. Follow along as we walk you through the milestones Spotify has hit.




2006: Spotify is founded

To start, this is Spotify's first website from 2006. Compared to today it is really simple and not as colorful. As you can see they were looking for employees as Spotify had just been founded and wouldn't be released to the public until 2008 and that would only be in Europe.





2008: Released in Europe




This next home page is from 2008. This was the year that Spotify was released to the public, but only in Europe. If you look in the top left you can see that the logo was still the full spotify word instead of the O it is today.





2011: Released to America




Finnaly in 2011 Spotify was released to America. This is the home page from that year. If you look in the middle of the page you can see that the Spotify app looked much different than today. Instead of everything having an image for it for the cover in the middle of the screen, every song or album is in a list on the page and the cover is in the corner.





2016: 100 Million Users




This page is from 2016 when Spotify hit the milestone of 100 million users! For most of the 2010's spotify went with a clean home page like this one and switched to just the O with the lines above for the logo. The fee for premium at this time was $9.99 a month.




2025: Present Day

Lastly, we have Spotify from today where it doesn't need to be downloaded and can be used from the browser. Now it looks almost identical to the app and has recomendations on the home page. The price for premium now is $11.99 per month.
